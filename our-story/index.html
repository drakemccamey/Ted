Our Story

A Hico connection. A dog named Ted.

Ted’s Kitchen is named in memory of Ted, owner Stephen Ferguson’s beloved dog. His name lives on here in downtown Hico, where we serve fresh smash burgers, classic hot dogs, crispy sides, and Henry’s Homemade Ice Cream.

For Stephen, a former Hico Tiger, the restaurant is a new chapter in a town that’s already part of his story. For our crew, it’s a chance to make Ted’s the kind of place you work into your everyday life: lunch with coworkers, dinner with the family, or an ice cream stop just because.

We keep the food straightforward and give the details our attention. Fresh, never-frozen beef smashed on the grill. Your favorite toppings. A scoop worth saving room for.

Grab a table inside or settle into Ted’s Backyard. Whether Hico is home or a stop along the way, we’d love to have you here.
